                          Accounts

                          Your banks, cards and wallets. Balance now is what's in each one today — change it to correct the app; only entries after that count on top of it. Numbers are the last 4 digits of the card or account as they appear in the bank's SMS.

                          Credit card

                          Already owe something on the card or have running installments? Add them as credit card purchases with their real date, then tap Installments on the purchase and set the first due date. Installments already billed are counted automatically.

                          Bank statements (PDF)

                          Choose your CIB credit card statements. The app adds the transactions it doesn't have yet, matches the ones it already found in your SMS, registers your active installments, and takes your card position (amount due, due date, balance, limit) from the newest statement. Nothing changes until you tap Import.

                          Choose statement PDFs

                          Payslips

                          Choose the payslip files from your email (.htm), or take a photo of a paper payslip (with a Claude key it is read for you; otherwise type the month and the net). The net paid is added as Salary to your debit account on the day you choose. A salary already found in your SMS or statement is matched, not added twice.

                          Choose payslip files (.htm)

                          Read bank SMS

                          Every bank SMS that mentions one of your numbers below is added automatically to the right card. Type the last 4 digits of each card and your account numbers, separated by commas.

                          🔔 Bank & wallet app notifications

                          Some cards and wallets (Telda, for example) send no SMS, only a notification in their app. Turn this on and the app reads only the notifications of the banks and wallets in your accounts, and records them on the right account. When the same money also comes by SMS, the two are joined into one entry.

                          Test a message

                          Google Drive backup

                          Sign in once and the app backs up every day to an "Expenses Backups" folder in your Google Drive. The newest 30 backups are kept.

                          Recurring payments

                          Bills you pay every month (building fees, internet, a loan…). On the day, Home shows them with a Paid button and you get a reminder. A payment in the same category that month (e.g. from SMS) counts as paid.

                            "Add it for me" is for payments that never come by SMS (cash, for example), so they aren’t counted twice.

                            Category budgets

                            A monthly limit for any expense category. Home → this month shows how much is left, and you get an alert at 80% and when you go over. Leave empty for no limit. The grey number is your average of the last 3 months.

                            Reminders

                            📱 Home-screen widget

                            Shows what you spent this month and what is left (per day when a goal or budget sets a limit). To add it: long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → Expenses → drag it to the screen. It updates whenever the app saves.

                            Profiles

                            Each profile has its own accounts and entries — for example your wife's or a small business. Bank SMS, app notifications, reminders, the widget and the Google Drive backup work in “Me” only; other profiles are kept by hand and with the Backup file.

                            Backup file

                            Save everything in one file, or restore from one. Restoring adds and updates entries; it never deletes anything.

                            The backup file includes your invoice photos and PDFs. The Google Drive backup includes them only when you switch that on in Google Drive backup.
